# Fantasy Basketball Dashboard — Product Specification

**League:** Big Baller League, Yahoo Fantasy Basketball 2026–27. **Status (October 21, 2026):** Phases 0–2, 3A–3C.1, 3D (read-only Yahoo integration), 3E (Waiver Advisor), 3E.1 (recommendation audit), 3E.2, 3F, 3G (three-view page), 4A–4B (Trade Lab) and 4D (market-driven trade opportunities) are implemented. The app is local and read-only; it never submits Yahoo transactions.

## Purpose and rules

Support the manager's decisions using actual league rules, auditable projection inputs, dated NBA games, legal lineups, and scheduled Yahoo opponents. Optimize category results rather than generic player ranks. Show source, as-of time, assumptions, and missing verification with every analysis.

The league has ten teams and nine H2H categories: FG%, FT%, 3PTM, PTS, REB, AST, STL, BLK, and TO. Lower TO wins. Each category contributes a weekly W/L/T; a 6–3 matchup adds six wins and three losses. Daily lineup slots are PG, SG, G, SF, PF, F, C, C, Util, Util, with four bench and three IL slots. League settings record four acquisitions per Yahoo scoring period, a one-day standard rolling waiver, and a cannot-cut list. The regular season covers 18 scoring periods; Weeks 7 and 17 span 14 days. Playoffs are configured for six teams in Weeks 19–21, but bracket assignments need independent verification before odds are shown.

## Implemented product

| Area | Current behavior |
|---|---|
| Inputs | Import Hashtag and LeagueWinner workbook sheets, validate source-specific shooting volumes, reconcile a complete dated Yahoo roster/player capture, and apply 35 reviewed player-ID decisions. Yahoo-displayed eligibility is used. |
| Deterministic core | Blend per-game makes, attempts, counting stats, and GP; assign legal daily starters; weight expected starts by projected GP/82; compute FG%/FT% from summed makes and attempts. |
| H2H dashboard | Validate all 90 regular-season pairings; forecast nine-category weekly results, cumulative 18-week W-L-T, win percentage, and seed. Display weekly results and Min-Max league-relative category strengths (100 best, 0 worst, 50 all tied). |
| Waiver Advisor | One read-only workflow. Every available player is scored as a first move; multi-move streams and permanent upgrades are searched within weekly budgets; up to five distinct strategies are ranked against HOLD by short-term category wins, flip fragility and rest-of-season roster value. Can't Cut, legal lineups, locks and timing are enforced. These are conditional forecasts, not verified executable transactions. |
| Trade Lab | Read-only trade ideas for my team only: 1-for-1, 2-for-1, 1-for-2 and 2-for-2 packages with every partner, replayed exactly for both teams with legal lineups, roster balancing and free-agent opportunity cost. Market value (how other managers see players: Yahoo preseason ADP plus FantasyPros consensus rankings) is kept apart from my competitive benefit (robust rest-of-season and next-three-week category wins). Paying the partner a market premium is allowed when it buys real category gains. A trade where the partner would lose more than 10% by market value is never shown; on a multi-player side the best player counts in full and the others at half. Each card carries one label (Market opportunity, Market-balanced, Mutually beneficial, Expensive improvement) from stated criteria. No acceptance probability is claimed and nothing is sent to Yahoo. |
| Recommendation audit | Each shown strategy has a collapsed audit: the contribution of each transaction (in order, left out, and alone), legal shorter plans, net permanent replacements with per-game stats, games, starts and scarcity, and Hashtag/LeagueWinner/Blended sensitivity with warnings. It explains ranking; it does not change it. |

The advisor ranks by projected horizon category points, a horizon margin score, and a rest-of-season term for the roster left behind, minus a small cost per acquisition. A flip that disappears under one projection source or a 5% margin shift counts half. Margin scores and sensitivity checks are ranking aids, not probabilities. Legacy 3A–3C.1 routes keep their own ranking contracts but no longer have dashboard panels.

## Architecture and source boundaries

`backend/app` is a Python analytic core and localhost HTTP server. `projections.py`, `matching.py`, `browser_players.py`, and `reviewed_matches.py` validate workbook and Yahoo identity data. `blending.py`, `schedule.py`, and `phase1.py` implement stat blending, dated fixtures, and legal daily lineups. `matchups.py` and `deterministic.py` produce scheduled season forecasts; `simulator.py` supplies shared actual-state/category helpers and a deferred Monte Carlo backend. `advisor.py` is the Waiver Advisor; `waiver.py` and `streaming.py` hold shared helpers and the legacy routes. `dashboard.py` serves the page and existing routes. Data sources are the original workbook and league settings, NBA and fantasy schedules, reviewed mappings, and dated private Yahoo captures. See [CURRENT_STATE.md](docs/CURRENT_STATE.md) for specific contracts.

Keep original inputs, private captures, and historical snapshot formats intact. No new player projection, injury status, transaction outcome, or NBA game date may be invented. A displayed FA or W label is a dated observation; it does not prove claim success or transaction timing.

## Limitations and roadmap

**Phase 3D–3F (implemented; verification gaps in CURRENT_STATE):** capture synchronized, authenticated, read-only Yahoo roster, pool, transaction and injury status evidence. Suppress only imminent games for players currently marked out/inactive; retain undated season GP expectations. Capture final completed-week matchup scores and cumulative standings with ten-team reconciliation, plus current-week started-slot final-game volumes. Reject incomplete or inconsistent automatic state and retain the manual actual-state contract. A successful refresh reloads the deterministic forecast and unified Waiver Advisor. Keep Yahoo writes out of scope.

**Deferred:** calibrate the preserved Monte Carlo backend before displaying weekly or playoff probabilities; verify the league-specific playoff bracket. Three-team trades and a calibrated market model remain future work. Later calibration and sourced injury/news enhancements remain future work.

**Known modeling gaps:** The saved Yahoo capture is dated and may be stale. Future ownership and waiver outcomes are conditional. Candidate IL eligibility and direct-IL placement are not generally verified. The actual-state contract carries completed category outcomes but not completed-week category stat totals. Thirty Cup/replacement NBA games lack supplied dates and are omitted; live postponements are not verified. Legal projected starts and GP/82 availability are estimates, not actual appearances. Yahoo status alone does not provide a return date; the imminent-game rule is deliberately limited to the capture day and following scoring day. The first completed-week rollover in this league has not yet been verified live. The advisor scores every first move, but multi-move combinations use a bounded beam that is exhaustive only in small fixtures. Trade market value is a heuristic curve over public preseason signals, not a market price; trade labels and rank weights are declared heuristics.
